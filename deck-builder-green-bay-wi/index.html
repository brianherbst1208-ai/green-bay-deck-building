Green Bay homeowners ask a lot of their decks. A good one has to shrug off lake-effect snow load, survive brutal freeze-thaw winters off the bay, and still look right on everything from an older Astor or east-side home to a new build out in Howard or Bellevue. That's the deck we build. As locally based deck builders, we design and construct custom decks across every Green Bay neighborhood — built for how you actually live outdoors, from quiet mornings on the water to game-day crowds.

Deck Building Built for Green Bay

Our winters are the real test. We set every footing below the local frost line so your deck doesn't heave, flash and bolt ledgers correctly to keep water out of the house, and use fasteners and framing rated for the snow load and freeze-thaw swings we get off the bay. On clay-soil and riverfront lots, that structural care is the difference between a deck that lasts decades and one that loosens in a few seasons.

Composite or Natural Wood?

We build in low-maintenance composite (Trex and TimberTech), classic cedar, and budget-friendly pressure-treated lumber — and we'll walk you through the tradeoffs in upkeep, lifespan, and cost so the choice fits your home and budget. Many Green Bay homeowners choose composite to skip the yearly staining our long winters and short summers make such a chore.
