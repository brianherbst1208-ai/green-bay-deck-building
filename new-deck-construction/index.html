A new deck is part design project, part structural build — and we handle both. We start at your home, measure the space, and design a deck around how you actually want to use it: dining and grilling, a hot-tub landing, a shaded lounge, or a multi-level walk-out that follows a sloped Northeast Wisconsin lot. You approve the layout, the materials, and an itemized price before we order a single board.

What's Included

  • Free on-site design consultation and measurements
  • Custom layout — single-level, multi-level, or wraparound
  • Permit drawings prepared and permits pulled
  • Frost-depth footings and code-compliant framing
  • Your choice of composite, cedar, or pressure-treated decking
  • Railings, stairs, lighting, and built-in options
  • Full site cleanup and a final inspection walkthrough

Engineered for Wisconsin Winters

Every build starts underground. We set footings below frost depth, size beams and joists properly, and flash the ledger so water stays out of your house — the structural details that keep a deck solid through decades of freeze-thaw and heavy lake-effect snow load. Then we frame, deck, and finish with your chosen material and hidden fasteners for a clean surface.

Permits Handled

Most Green Bay- and Fox Valley-area decks require a building permit. We prepare the drawings, pull the permit, and build to code so your final inspection is a formality — not a surprise. Call (920) 710-3952 to start with a free on-site design consultation.
