Why Local Matters

Northeast Wisconsin-Built Decks That Last

Lake-effect snow load, deep frost, freeze-thaw cycles, and clay soils are hard on a deck. We set footings below frost depth, flash ledgers correctly, and use fasteners and materials rated for our climate — so your deck stays solid and safe for decades.
