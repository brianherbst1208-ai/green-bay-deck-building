Why Homeowners Choose Us

Northeast Wisconsin-Built Decks That Last

Lake-effect snow load, deep frost, freeze-thaw cycles, and clay soils are hard on a deck. We set footings below frost depth, flash ledgers correctly, and use fasteners and materials rated for our climate — so your deck stays solid and safe for decades.

  • Insured & permitted — licensed builds with proper permits and frost-depth footings that pass inspection.
  • Composite & natural wood — Trex, TimberTech, cedar, and pressure-treated, matched to your budget and upkeep.
  • Clear, itemized quotes — you approve the design and the price before a single board is cut.
  • Built for our winters — framing and footings engineered for lake-effect snow load, freeze-thaw, and clay soils.
